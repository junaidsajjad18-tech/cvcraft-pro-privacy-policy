CVCraft Pro

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

CVCraft Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CVCraft Pro mobile application.

CVCraft Pro is designed to help users create professional CVs, resumes, cover letters, and PDF documents.

1. Information You Enter

CVCraft Pro allows you to enter information into your CV or cover letter, which may include:

  • Name and contact information
  • Professional summary
  • Work experience
  • Education
  • Skills
  • Projects
  • Certifications
  • Languages
  • Achievements and awards
  • Courses and training
  • References
  • Other information you choose to include in your CV

This information is provided voluntarily by you for the purpose of creating and editing your documents.

2. Profile Photos

CVCraft Pro may allow you to select or capture a profile photo for use in supported CV templates.

Photos selected or captured by you are used for your CV and are not intentionally uploaded to our servers by CVCraft Pro.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

CVCraft Pro is designed to process and store your CV information locally on your device.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores your personal CV information as part of the core CV creation functionality.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for any files that you choose to export, save, share, or upload to other services.

4. PDF Documents

CVCraft Pro can generate PDF documents from the information you provide.

Generated PDF files are created for you to save, share, print, or use for job applications. When you choose to save or share a PDF, the file may be handled by the Android system or another application selected by you.

CVCraft Pro does not control how third-party applications handle files after you choose to share them.

5. Data Collection and Sharing

CVCraft Pro does not intentionally collect or sell your personal CV information to third parties.

We do not intentionally share your CV information, profile photo, cover letters, or generated documents with third parties as part of the core functionality of the application.

6. Internet Connection

Core CV creation functionality is designed to work locally on your device where supported.

Some functionality provided by Android, Google Play, or other services may require an internet connection. Such services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Third-Party Services

CVCraft Pro may use services provided by third parties in the future, such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, payment, or other application services.

If such services are introduced and they collect or process user data, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe the relevant data practices.

8. Advertising

At the time of this Privacy Policy, CVCraft Pro does not intentionally collect personal CV information for advertising purposes.

If advertising services are added to the application in the future, the Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play data disclosures will be updated as necessary.

9. Children's Privacy

CVCraft Pro is a general productivity application intended for users creating professional CVs and job application documents. It is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core functionality of the application.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to design the application so that personal CV information can be handled locally on the user's device.

However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their device, operating system, and exported documents protected.

11. Your Control Over Your Information

You control the information you enter into CVCraft Pro. You can edit or remove your CV information through the application and can manage exported documents through your device's file management system.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, functionality, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of CVCraft Pro, please use the contact information provided on the CVCraft Pro Google Play listing.

14. Acceptance

By using CVCraft Pro, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
